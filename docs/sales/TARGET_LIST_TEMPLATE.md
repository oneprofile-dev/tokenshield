Goal: 20 cold contacts, 5 booked calls, 1 signed pilot by day 14.

## How to use this file

1. Fill in 20 rows below — real names, real companies, real reasons to reach out
2. Send one message per day for 10 working days using scripts from `OUTREACH_SCRIPTS.md`
3. Update the status column daily
4. Review every Friday: response rate, common objections, who's worth bumping

## Suggested sources (fill the list in priority order)

### Tier 1 — warmest leads (start here, target: 5 contacts)
- People in your existing CuratedMCP newsletter / Discord who are eng leaders
- Friends-of-friends who run AI-forward eng teams
- Anyone who shared/commented on your TokenShield posts

### Tier 2 — public signals (target: 10 contacts)
- X search: `(claude code OR cursor) (expensive OR cost OR bill)` → recent posts with 5+ likes
- r/ClaudeAI top posts past 30 days → comment authors who run teams
- HN comments past month on Anthropic threads → engineering leaders

### Tier 3 — cold but qualified (target: 5 contacts)
- LinkedIn search "VP Engineering" posted about "AI tooling" in 30d
- ProductHunt makers of dev tools in last 90 days
- YC company directories — recent batches, AI-adjacent companies

## Status legend

- **New** — not yet sent
- **Sent D0** — initial message out
- **Bumped D4** — soft follow-up sent
- **Bumped D10** — final follow-up sent
- **Replied** — got a response, see Notes
- **Booked** — 15-min call scheduled
- **Closed-won** — pilot agreement signed, invoice paid
- **Closed-lost** — passed (capture reason in Notes)
- **Ghosted** — no reply after 3 messages, dropped

## Weekly review questions

Every Friday for the first 4 weeks, ask yourself:

1. **Response rate** — what % replied? (Target: 25–40% for warm channels, 8–15% for cold)
2. **Hook quality** — which "Hook" column entries got replies? Pattern?
3. **Common objection** — what's the #1 reason for "Closed-lost"? (This is your pricing/positioning fix)
4. **Channel ROI** — DMs vs email vs LinkedIn — which converted best?
5. **Time-to-call** — how long from first send to booked call?

After 50 sends, if response rate is <8%, the message is wrong. Rewrite. Don't send 50 more first.
